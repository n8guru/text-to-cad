# Step 10 camera, lighting, and wiring integration

## Delivered CAD authority

- A Step 6-derived shell with real provisional passages for the rear camera
  housing and high-mounted brake-light recess.
- A centered relocated-camera housing plus a visible rearward optical
  keep-out for obstruction review.
- A centered high-mounted brake-light bar and an interior driver-service
  envelope.
- Two symmetric interior task-light envelopes.
- A connected mechanical harness package: driver-side base raceway, rear
  riser, header branch, camera drop, and service junction.
- A vehicle-ingress reservation labeled `PROTOCOL_UNKNOWN` and
  `PLACEHOLDER_REQUIRING_SCAN`.

## Authority boundary

This package is authoritative only for provisional mechanical volume,
location, access, routing, and keep-out intent. It does not define or claim
Rivian protocol compatibility, connectors, pinout, voltage, current, wire
gauge, circuit protection, grounding, network behavior, diagnostics, optical
calibration, photometry, legal compliance, waterproofing, thermal performance,
EMC, functional safety, production fitment, or fabrication readiness.

The Step 6 source shell remains unchanged. Step 10 is a parallel package to
Steps 7–9; Step 11 must compose the independently verified packages and
engineer hardpoints, laminate, service access, assembly, and structural checks.
Step 13 owns target-vehicle calibration and Step 14 owns fabrication release.
