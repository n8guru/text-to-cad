# Step 9 official interface sources and authority boundary

The target is Nate's first-generation 2022 R1T. These official Rivian sources
support product intent, not production geometry:

- [2022 R1T Owner's Guide](https://assets.rivian.com/2md5qhoeajym/530xs9hu11xOKefT2JrvA5/76d493c33659e9ceac1d72175ec54f26/r1t-owners-guide-en-us-20221017.pdf)
  states that the roof and truck-bed rails have accessory ports and describes
  the crossbar's two-stage paddle latch. It does not publish a transferable
  port profile or authorize a third-party composite hardpoint.
- [Sizing up the R1T](https://stories.rivian.com/sizing-up-the-r1t)
  states that four bed-rail tie-downs double as Cargo Crossbar mounting points
  and identifies bed-rail controls for the tailgate and powered tonneau.
- [Current R1 Cargo Crossbars](https://gearshop.rivian.com/products/cargo-crossbars)
  confirms the current product is telescoping and snaps into accessory ports.
  Its advertised vehicle ratings and dimensional range are not adopted as
  topper ratings and do not establish first-generation port geometry.

Accordingly, Step 9 models only bounded envelopes:

- four factory-anchor receivers at the existing scan-required anchor points;
- four topper-roof accessory-port placeholders at two selected shell stations;
- removable bridge/load-spreader packaging between anchors and base flanges;
- non-electronic source couplers, push-pull route corridors, and side pods for
  the obstructed tonneau and tailgate controls;
- service and vehicle-motion keep-outs.

Every mating profile, fastener, latch, control stroke, local surface normal,
clearance, and permissible load remains `PLACEHOLDER REQUIRING SCAN`. Step 11
must engineer laminate/hardpoints and capacity. Step 13 must calibrate the
interfaces on the target truck. No Rivian endorsement or OEM compatibility is
claimed.
