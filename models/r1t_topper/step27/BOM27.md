## 4. What is NOT resolved — these block ordering

- **bulb_seal** (ORDERABLE) — Trim-Lok publishes no compression-force curve for the DD co-extruded family. If a force number is needed, the Specialty Trim Seal combos (e.g. 105B100B3X1/8A at 0.822 lb/in at 25 %) are the only bulb-on-channel products with published force data. DD6138/DD6139 are catalogue/RFQ parts with no web cart - order through a rep.
- **chmsl** (ORDERABLE) — Nothing SAE-J1957/U3-marked exists in the 250-350 mm window originally wanted; 394 mm is the shortest compliant bar found. Confirm the U3 marking on the physical lens before cutting the pocket, and resolve the housing-material conflict between distributors.
- **compression_limiter** (CUSTOM) — McMaster part numbers could not be resolved (mcmaster.com now requires a login for all product tables). The U.S. Plastic 3/8 in sheet item number still needs confirming.
- **gas_strut** (SERIES_ONLY) — The 'Dia 10 ball' convention is ambiguous: DIN 71802/71803 pairs a Dia 10 ball with M6, the gas-spring industry pairs it with M8x1.25. Our drawing must state M8 explicitly. Also, NO 316L stainless spring offers a 150 mm stroke - the nearest is Stabilus 9055RV at 140 mm.
- **harness_tap** (ORDERABLE) — Pin count / pinout NOT published by Rivian. Resolve from the Aptiv catalogue for 13897598 or Service Manual LC 781013010 before releasing the harness drawing.
- **hinge** (ORDERABLE) — PDF vs HTML dimension conflict at size 50: PDF gives d=6.5 / h1=10, the HTML table gives 6.6 / 11.9. PDF treated as authoritative; verify before cutting.
- **hinge** — GN 7233 is a MULTI-JOINT LINKAGE, not a fixed-pivot butt hinge. There is no single hinge-axis offset; the leaf follows a coupler curve. Our CAD models a PURE ROTATION about a nominal design axis as the design intent, and checks that the leaf stays inside the published installation-space envelope (l4 x l5, radius r, offsets x/y). The true path MUST be re-checked against JW Winco's free 2D/3D CAD before any drawing is released. This is a named OPEN item, not a solved one.
- **locating_pin_diamond** (SERIES_ONLY) — MISUMI's (W) column gives 4 mm at D=10, but whether W is across-flats or the remaining land width is not certified in the archived page. 4 mm across-flats on a 10 mm head is implausible, so it is read as land width - confirm on the live drawing.
- **locating_pin_round** (SERIES_ONLY) — Dash number is not final until plate thickness L, workpiece thickness B and material prefix are fixed. Re-verify the p6 cell at D=10 on the live page (MISUMI was in a 24 h outage during this pass).
- **release_cable** (SERIES_ONLY) — Exact length/extension digits and confirmation of the fitting letters must come from Southco's configurator - one live SKU contradicts the printed fitting table.
- **t_handle** (ORDERABLE) — No keyed T-handle exists in stainless E3. Marine-grade corrosion resistance AND a key is not available in this series: accept zinc/black powder or change series. The CH751 code comes from the 2011 catalogue revision and the current PDF omits it - confirm with Southco.
